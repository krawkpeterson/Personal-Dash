01 — Game

KRAWK

Your zip code. Their graveyard.

A browser FPS fought on real ground. Type any US zip and the map is built from public elevation, every street, every building, and the weather outside right now. Two guns, synthesized from scratch. A drill sergeant who grades you. It runs in the tab.

02 — Systems

Zip to warzone.

About twenty seconds. Then instant.

Geocode, elevation tiles, OpenStreetMap, and live weather run in parallel. A director writes the battle. On the public build, a rules engine does the same job. The archive is gzipped and sharded, so the second visit does not wait. One pipeline. It runs on a server and in the browser.

  • Express
  • SQLite shards
  • Open data
  • Strict TypeScript
